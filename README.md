# Bitcoin-Sentiment-Analysis
## Bitcoin Market Sentiment & Trader Performance Analysis

## Project Description

- This project analyzes the relationship between Bitcoin market sentiment and trader performance using historical trading data and the Fear & Greed Index. The objective is to identify hidden trading patterns, evaluate trader profitability under different market conditions, and generate actionable insights that support smarter trading strategies.

- The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business-focused analytics, and an interactive Power BI dashboard for data visualization.

## Domain

- Financial Data Analytics
- Cryptocurrency Analytics
- Trading Analytics

## Project Objective

- Analyze trader performance under different market sentiment conditions.
- Explore the relationship between market sentiment and trading profitability.
- Uncover hidden patterns in trading behavior.
- Compare BUY and SELL performance across different market sentiments.
- Evaluate coin-wise profitability under various market conditions.
- Generate business insights and recommend data-driven trading strategies.

## Key Insights

- Neutral market sentiment achieved the highest win rate and profit factor.
- Fear market conditions also resulted in strong trading performance.
- Extreme Greed showed the weakest trading performance.
- BUY and SELL profitability varied across different market sentiments.
- Trading fees differed under different market conditions.
- Coin profitability changed significantly depending on market sentiment.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize trading during Neutral and Fear market conditions.
- Exercise caution during Extreme Greed due to lower profitability.
- Select BUY or SELL strategies based on prevailing market sentiment.
- Monitor transaction fees to improve overall profitability.
- Choose cryptocurrencies according to market sentiment trends.
- Combine market sentiment with effective risk management techniques.
